## Status authority

- `READY`: dependencies and research gates permit the next bounded slice.
- `BLOCKED`: a named dependency or product decision prevents safe work.
- `DISCOVERY_REQUIRED`: customer evidence is required before implementation planning.
- `DONE`: DeepSeek completed a slice with evidence.
- `READY FOR INDEPENDENT SOL VERIFICATION`: implementer verification is complete.
- `PASS`, `SAFE TO CLOSE`, and `CLOSED`: Sol-only decisions.

DeepSeek may update checkpoints and add compact receipts. It may not promote a
blocked milestone around a dependency, approve its own work, or mark later
milestones READY merely because code was written.
